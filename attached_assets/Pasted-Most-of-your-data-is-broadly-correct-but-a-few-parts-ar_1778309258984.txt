# 1. Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

### Status: Mostly correct, but needs updates

### Correct

* Central benefit amount: ₹5,000 for first living child ✔
* Age 19+ ✔
* Aadhaar + bank account ✔
* Registration through AWC/ASHA ✔
* MCP card required ✔

### Needs correction/update

#### A) First child only → NOT fully accurate anymore

Under PMMVY 2.0:

* First child → ₹5,000
* Second child → ₹6,000 IF the second child is a girl child

This was revised under Mission Shakti guidelines. ([PMMVY][1])

So your database should say:

> “Benefit available for first living child. Additional benefit for second child if the second child is a girl child (PMMVY 2.0).”

---

#### B) Installment structure outdated

Your app says:

* Form 1A
* Form 1B
* Form 1C
* 3 installments

That was older PMMVY workflow.

Current PMMVY 2.0 implementation in many states:

* ₹5,000 is often released in 2 installments instead of 3. ([PMMVY][1])

Because implementation varies slightly state-to-state, safest wording is:

> “Installments may vary by state/implementation phase.”

---

#### C) Husband Aadhaar may no longer be mandatory

Recent updates indicate husband Aadhaar requirement has been relaxed/removed in newer PMMVY workflows. ([PMMVY][1])

So instead of:

> “Aadhaar (mother + husband)”

Use:

> “Mother’s Aadhaar mandatory; husband Aadhaar may be requested in some states.”

---

#### D) URL

Your URL is correct:

[PMMVY Official Portal](https://pmmvy.wcd.gov.in?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

# 4. Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

### Status: Partially accurate but oversimplified

### Important correction

JSY amounts differ between:

* Low Performing States (LPS)
* High Performing States (HPS)
* Rural vs Urban

Your current wording:

> ₹1,400 rural / ₹1,000 urban

This only applies to LPS states.

You should instead structure it like:

| Category  | Amount |
| --------- | ------ |
| Rural LPS | ₹1,400 |
| Urban LPS | ₹1,000 |
| Rural HPS | ₹700   |
| Urban HPS | ₹600   |

This is important for a pan-India app.

---

### Also important

Eligibility rules differ between LPS and HPS:

* In many HPS states, BPL/SC/ST restrictions apply
* In LPS states, all pregnant women delivering in govt facilities may qualify

So:

> “Must belong to BPL family”

is not universally accurate nationwide.

Better wording:

> “Eligibility varies by state category (LPS/HPS) and socio-economic status.”

---

#### URL

Instead of the incomplete URL:

> [https://nhm.gov.in/](https://nhm.gov.in/)...

Use:

[National Health Mission](https://nhm.gov.in?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
